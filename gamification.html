Gamification of Life

Turn everyday life into meaningful play: worlds where your choices ripple across virtual ecosystems and the real one too.

Play your part in shaping tomorrow

G.A.J.R.A. Earth

G.A.J.R.A. Earth is not just an association, it's a game and a visionary platform where your actions can shape the future of our planet. Engage in interactive challenges, make decisions that ripple across virtual ecosystems, and witness the global impact of collective strategic play. As you navigate through the G.A.J.R.A. Earth experience, your choices contribute to a larger narrative, influencing virtual and real-world outcomes. Join a community committed to Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth and become an agent of change. Visit gajra.earth.

Reaching over one million people

Millionaire Aura

The Millionaire Aura isn't a measure of wealth but a testament to the power of collective action. It's about creating a ripple effect that touches over a million lives, sparking change and fostering a community dedicated to social and environmental good. As we build networks that connect millions, each individual becomes a beacon for change, contributing to a global movement that values people and planet. Our Millionaire Aura represents a commitment to unity, inspiring action, and transformative impact, proving that together, we are an unstoppable force for positive change.

Reaching over one billion people

Billionaire Aura

Billionaire Aura takes you into the stratosphere of high-stakes business strategy and philanthropy. Craft your empire and make philanthropic choices that echo through the annals of your virtual dynasty. From innovative start-ups to monumental charity projects, every choice expands your influence. This immersive experience teaches the balance between wealth accumulation and social impact, inviting players to redefine what it means to be a billionaire in today's world.

Survive, adapt, thrive: the cycle continues

Earth Catastrophe Cycles

Earth Catastrophe Cycles is an epic survival game that challenges you to withstand the trials of nature's most extreme events. Manage resources, construct resilient communities, and lead recovery efforts in the aftermath of environmental upheavals. As you progress, you'll unlock the secrets of past civilisations, learning from their successes and failures. Discover the strength in unity and innovation, and emerge victorious as you navigate through the cycles of regeneration.

Unravel mysteries, reveal ancient cosmic connections

Ancient Aliens

Delve into the enigmatic world of Ancient Aliens, where history and myth intertwine. Explore ancient sites, decipher extraterrestrial clues, and unlock the secrets of civilisations long gone. This game is a journey through time, blending archaeological discovery with the thrill of uncovering otherworldly influences on Earth's past. Connect the dots of the universe's greatest mysteries and reveal truths that have been hidden for eons.

Dive into the crystal city of Quandamooka Country

Shifting Sands of a Timeless Land

Shifting Sands of a Timeless Land is the inaugural chapter of an epic narrative set in the mystical subterranean crystal city of Quandamooka Country. Journey beneath the sub-tropical waters of Brisbane's Moreton Bay, gliding past North Stradbroke Island to the edge of the continental shelf. Here lies a tale of ancient lore and futuristic visions, a saga where the past interlaces with the possible. As you delve into this narrative, experience the profound connection between the land and its people, the ebb and flow of time, and the enduring spirit of a civilisation as resplendent and enduring as the stars above.
