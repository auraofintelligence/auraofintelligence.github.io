Events & Event Management

Build the event software that's actually yours

Most event software is something you rent: built for everyone, shaped to no one, and never quite what your gathering needs. Here you do it differently. Use this as a place to think about what an event really asks of you, the tools that already exist and the ones that don't yet, and build your own: a calendar, guest management, real-time updates, whatever your gathering genuinely calls for. It's yours to keep, custom to how you bring people together, and it grows as you do. Intimate rooms, grand galas, or something globally synchronised: no subscription to big tech, your guest list stays yours, and the whole thing stays true to joyful responsible abundance.
