Pathways / Mind & self

Mind Palace Builder

A place to keep what matters, and one you own. Turn your own photos into rooms, build or buy the architecture, furnish it, and direct the scenes that tell your story inside it. It's not a template you rent; it's your palace, and it keeps growing as your life does.

3D rooms from photos & videos

Your memories, made walkable

Upload your own photos and videos and watch them become rooms you can walk through. These are your memories rebuilt as living spaces inside your palace: moments from the past, places in the present, and rooms you set up ready for what's ahead. They stay on your side, yours to revisit whenever you like.

Buy the blueprint, or draw your own

Build or purchase 3D buildings

Choose a structure from a gallery of grand designs, or draw your own from the ground up. Either way you end up with a building that's genuinely yours: a permanent home for your ideas rather than a space rented back to you. Each one is a lasting part of the palace you're building to keep.

Conceive. Create. Consecrate.

Design your own mind palace

Designing your own mind palace makes you the architect of your inner world. Draft the halls of thought, sculpt gardens of growth, and raise pillars of purpose exactly where you want them. Nothing here is off the shelf: the whole shape of it comes from you, and it stays as unique as you are while it evolves alongside you.

Furnish it your way

3D furnishings model generation

Furnish your palace with pieces you generate and choose yourself. Curate each room with objects that resonate with you: a chair that means something, an artefact of your own making, a small comfort for your quiet corners. It's your space to decorate however feels right, and to rearrange whenever you change.

Direct your own scenes

Scene development & cameras

You're the director here, telling your own story in your own space. Angle the camera to capture your victories, your passions, the people you love and the legacy you're leaving. You script and stage the scenes yourself, and the film they make belongs entirely to you.
