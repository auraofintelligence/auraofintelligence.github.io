Virtually sculpting reality

Earth models

Delve into the art of digital Earth sculpting with our interface. Here, you can manipulate terrain, experiment with ecosystems, and simulate natural events. Create your Earth, from serene landscapes to wild, untamed nature. With each adjustment, watch as your virtual world comes alive, mirroring the complexities of our planet. Our tools are designed to be intuitive yet powerful, making every aspect of Earth's majesty yours to edit and manipulate.

Craft celestial bodies, orbit into imagination

Planetary & moon models

Embark on a cosmic journey as you design your own celestial spheres. Shape craters on moons, carve valleys on alien worlds, and spin rings around gas giants. Our interface offers a universe of possibilities, allowing you to bring to life the planetary and lunar surfaces of your dreams. Whether for education or entertainment, our models provide a window into the wonders of space exploration.

Ignite stellar creativity, illuminate the cosmos

Solar & stellar models

Step into the role of a cosmic architect with tools to forge stars and ignite solar flares. From calm red dwarfs to raging supernovae, our interface gives you the power to simulate the lifecycle of stars. Adjust their luminosity, define their spectral class, and witness the birth and death of these celestial giants. It's not just a model; it's a dynamic storytelling canvas set against the vast backdrop of the universe.

Orchestrate orbits, harmonise a system

Solar system models

The symphony of our solar system and others await your direction. Align planets, choreograph their orbits, and set the stage for meteor showers. With our Solar System Builder, you become the maestro of celestial mechanics, balancing the delicate interplay of gravity and motion. Educators, enthusiasts and creators alike can use our platform to simulate and study the dance of our cosmic neighbourhood.

Weave the fabric of the cosmos

Galaxy models

Spin galaxies and thread the fabric of the universe with our Galaxy Builder. Spiral arms, glowing nebulae, and pulsating quasars are at your fingertips, allowing for creations that rival the beauty of the night sky. Whether you're a storyteller setting a scene among the stars or an educator demonstrating the vast scales of our universe, our Galaxy Models provide a sandbox of astronomical proportions.
