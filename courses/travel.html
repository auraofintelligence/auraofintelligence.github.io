Travel: plan your own way

Build your own way to travel: a trip atlas, a compass that reads the live signals, and a tribe gathered around the journey before there's ever a place.

What you'll build

Travel tools that are yours

Most travel apps want to sit between you and the world, take a cut, and keep your history. This course is the other way round: you build your own trip atlas, your own oracle that reads the live signals and points to the next right place, and your own way of gathering the people before the plans. The tools are yours, and they get better every trip.

It follows the shape of Luke's own big dream, Project Odyssey: years on the road, arriving useful, leaving tools behind. You don't need his itinerary; you need your own, built to fit how you actually want to move.
