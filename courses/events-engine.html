Plan community events

This is the events engine I'm working to start on Minjerribah, out in the open, with AI as my only co-worker so far. It isn't running yet; it needs people. Here's what I'm building, and how you'd start your own, wherever you are.

What I'm building here

An events atlas for Quandamooka Country, consent-first

This is two simple things working together: a public-safe atlas of what's on, and a plain Markdown builder for planning a gathering from first idea to run sheet. It's shaped for events on Quandamooka Country, so consent comes first: nothing sensitive gets mapped, nothing gets shared that shouldn't be. I'm putting the whole thing together as a lab you can look right inside: the plan, the tools, the moving parts. Because I'm doing it on my own with AI, it stays a description ahead of a crowd until the island gets involved. It only becomes real the moment people show up to build it with me.

If you're on Straddie, that's the open invitation: come and help ground it. If you're anywhere else, read on.

Build your own, wherever you are

Same idea, shaped to your place

The reason it lives here, on Aura of Intelligence, is so it's more than my island's events. Take it as a worked example and build your own: same idea, shaped to your town, your calendar, your gatherings and the way your community likes to meet. Not a copy of mine, yours, custom to your place and your lived experience.

That's the whole point of building your own tools and systems instead of renting someone else's. You keep what you make, and it grows the way your community does. The short video walks through how I'm approaching it, so you can borrow what fits and leave the rest.
