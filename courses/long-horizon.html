Big time horizons, built to keep

Some things won't pay off this year, or this decade. That's not a reason to leave them to whoever has the deepest pockets. This course looks at the furthest-out work with a straight face: mineral moonshots, and the deep-time frontier where a civilisation plans in centuries. It's about the models, the plans and the digital twins you can build and own now, so the long game starts on your terms rather than a corporation's.

The same thesis holds all the way out here: you build it, you keep it, and the small tools you own today are the seeds of the enormous ones later. Nothing about a long horizon means handing the future to big tech to rent back to you.
