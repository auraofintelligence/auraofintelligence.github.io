Straddie: grants, tenders & projects

Real grants, real tenders, real island projects: how to write the ones that land and start the ones that matter, with tools you build and keep rather than an agency you pay by the hour.

What you'll build

Your own grant and project kit

Grants and tenders have a shape to them. Once you can see it, you can write submissions that actually move money and permission toward the things your community needs. This course walks the real ones Luke has written from the island, and shows you how to build your own reusable kit: the templates, the checklists, the evidence, all yours to keep and adapt.

Then it turns to starting things: the small, concrete island projects that begin as a lab and grow into something a community can steer. You own the tools you build here, so the next submission and the next project are faster than the last.
