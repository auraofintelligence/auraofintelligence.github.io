Grants & tenders

How to write grants and tenders that actually land, using a reusable kit you build and own rather than an agency you pay by the hour. The real ones from the island, and how to write your own wherever you are.

What you'll build

Your own grants and tenders kit

Grants and tenders are two different doors. Grants are funding programs: someone offering money for the right idea. Tenders are buying processes: government asking a capable team to provide goods, services or works. Both have a shape to them, and once you can see it, you can put together submissions that actually move money and permission toward the things your community needs. This course walks the real ones Luke has written from Minjerribah, and shows you how to build your own reusable kit: the templates, the checklists, the evidence, all yours to keep and adapt.

You own the kit you build here, so the next submission is faster than the last, and it stays yours no matter where you are or what you're applying for. Not a service you hire, a skill you keep.
