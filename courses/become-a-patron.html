How patronage works here, and how to become one: back Luke's work and travels, and get something real in return. Value for value, out in the open.

How it works

Value for value, in the daylight

None of this runs on renting your attention or selling your data. It runs on patronage: people who back the work directly, and get something real back for it, songs, tools, dispatches from the road, and a public record of exactly what their support moved. This course walks you through the shapes that backing can take, from a one-off to a steady patron to a right-place sponsor.

It's also a working model you can borrow. If you make things and want to be funded without handing yourself to a platform, the way Right Place, Right Time is built is yours to study and adapt.
