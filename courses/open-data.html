Open your community's data

This is the open data project I'm working to start on Minjerribah, out in the open, with AI as my only co-worker so far. It isn't finished yet; it needs people. Here's what I'm building, and how you'd start your own, wherever you are.

What I'm building here

Evidence anyone can check, for one real question

This one takes a single real local question, the Dunwich (Gumpi) ferry terminal, and works it honestly with open data and a simple simulation. The aim isn't a slick answer; it's community-readable evidence anyone can open, follow and check for themselves. I'm putting the whole thing together as a lab you can look right inside: the data, the model, the workings. Because I'm doing it on my own with AI, it stays a description ahead of a crowd until the island gets involved. It only becomes real the moment people show up to build it with me.

If you're on Straddie, that's the open invitation: come and help ground it. If you're anywhere else, read on.

Build your own, wherever you are

Same idea, shaped to your place

The reason it lives here, on Aura of Intelligence, is so it's more than my island's ferry question. Take it as a worked example and build your own: same idea, pointed at the real question your community keeps asking, with your data, your model and evidence your neighbours can check. Not a copy of mine, yours, custom to your place and your lived experience.

That's the whole point of building your own tools and systems instead of renting someone else's. You keep what you make, and it grows the way your community does. The short video walks through how I'm approaching it, so you can borrow what fits and leave the rest.
