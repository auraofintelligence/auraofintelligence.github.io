The founder

Luke Nathan Hayes

"I wanted to build a digital bridge to my infinite self that I could wear as a mixed reality aura that imitates chakras."

I believe in infinite intelligence, and in the ability of a human being to create a computer-enabled, mixed reality light field interface with it. This whole site grew out of that one belief.

I recorded the first version of this idea in Brisbane in December 2022, not long after eleven months in India and a visit to Lumbini, Nepal. I live on North Stradbroke Island now, Quandamooka Country. You can find my day-job systems work at lukecatalyst.com or on LinkedIn.
